Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 25, 2026

At Unicode Cleaner, accessible from https://unicodecleaner.online, protecting user privacy is a fundamental priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how we protect user data.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing

Unicode Cleaner is architected to operate entirely within your browser.

  • No server-side processing. Text, JSON, or code snippets you paste into the tool are never transmitted to our servers.
  • No storage of user-submitted text. We do not log, save, or retain any content you process.
  • No data recovery. Once you close or refresh the page, your input is permanently cleared from your browser session.

This architectural design ensures maximum privacy and eliminates risks associated with uploading sensitive data.

2. Information We Collect

While we do not collect user-submitted content, certain technical information is automatically collected for website functionality and analytics.

2.1 Log Data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may collect standard log information including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps
  • Referring/exit pages
  • Number of clicks

This information is not linked to personally identifiable data and is used for security monitoring and performance optimization.

2.2 Analytics

We use Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand traffic patterns and feature usage (such as the JSON Cleaner and ChatGPT Cleaner tools). Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Unicode Cleaner uses cookies to:

  • Store user preferences
  • Improve site performance
  • Enable analytics
  • Support advertising services

Cookies do not give us access to your device or any personal data beyond what you choose to share.

4. Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Unicode Cleaner does not control third-party advertising cookies.

5. Google Analytics

Google Analytics may collect information such as browser type, pages visited, and session duration. This data helps us improve the tool and user experience.

You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Data Security

Because we do not process or store user-submitted text, sensitive content remains on your device. We implement reasonable technical safeguards for infrastructure and analytics data.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

8. Children’s Privacy

Unicode Cleaner does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable laws (GDPR, CCPA), including rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data. Since we do not collect user-submitted content or identifiable personal data, these rights are generally limited to analytics-related data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
