DETECTION SUMMARY

What is a Unicode Cleaner?

A Unicode Cleaner helps detect and remove hidden characters like zero-width spaces (U+200B), non-breaking spaces (U+00A0), byte order marks (U+FEFF), and other invisible Unicode characters. These hidden characters often break code, forms, JSON files, databases, and programming scripts.

Why Remove Zero-Width Characters?

Zero-width characters are invisible but can cause syntax errors, validation failures, and unexpected bugs. Developers often encounter these issues when copying text from AI tools, PDFs, Word documents, or websites.
